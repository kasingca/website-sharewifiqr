Pricing questions

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. Direct codes are free to create and download, and you can make as many as you need.

Do direct codes ever expire?

No. The network details are inside the code itself, so it keeps working for as long as your network name and password stay the same.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade from your account in the portal.
