Pricing questions

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. Direct codes are free to create and download, and you can make as many as you need.

Do direct codes ever expire?

No. The network details are inside the code itself, so it keeps working for as long as your network name and password stay the same.

What happens if I go over my monthly scans?

Your codes keep working. A printed card should never stop because it got popular. We email you when you reach 80% and 100% of your monthly tracked scans.

If your account uses more than twice its allowance for two billing months in a row, we will ask you to move to a plan that fits. If you stay over, we may limit tracked scans on your account until the next billing month or until you upgrade. Guests who scan a limited code are asked to get the Wi-Fi details from their host.

What counts as a tracked scan?

Each time someone opens a tracked code's Wi-Fi page. Bots and other automated traffic are not counted and may be blocked. Direct codes are never counted because scanning them never reaches us.

I need more than 10,000 scans a month

Large venues and events can ask us for a custom allowance from the portal. Until then, your codes keep working under the fair use policy.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade from your account in the portal.
