Frequently asked questions

How Wi-Fi QR codes work, and how to get the most out of yours.

Getting started

What's the difference between a direct code and a tracked code?

A direct code stores your network name and password inside the QR code, so a phone camera offers to join the network straight away. A tracked code stores a short Share Wifi QR link instead. Scanning it opens a page showing your network and password, and each scan is counted in your portal. You can also change a tracked code's network details without reprinting it.

Do my guests need an app?

No. The built-in camera app reads QR codes on iPhone (iOS 11 and later) and on most Android phones (Android 10 and later). On older Android phones, Google Lens does the same job.

Which security type should I choose?

Most home and business routers use WPA2 or WPA3, so choose WPA/WPA2/WPA3. Choose WEP only for very old routers, and None for open networks with no password. The type is usually shown in your router's settings or on its label.

Does it work with hidden networks?

Yes. Tick Hidden network when you create the code, and phones will look for your network even though it doesn't broadcast its name.

Can I print the code?

Yes. Download it as an image or a print-ready card. Print it at least 3 cm (just over an inch) wide, dark on a light background, and test the print with a phone before you frame it.

Security and privacy

Is it safe to put my Wi-Fi password in a QR code?

Anyone who can scan the code can join your network, just as anyone who can read a password card can. Put codes where only your guests will see them, and use your router's guest network if it has one, so visitors stay apart from your own computers and devices.

Do you store my Wi-Fi password?

For tracked codes, yes: we keep the network details so we can show them to guests who scan. Delete the code and they are deleted with it. A direct code carries the details inside the QR image, so scanning it never contacts us.

What do you record when someone scans a tracked code?

We count the scan and note when it happened. We don't ask guests for their name, email or phone number.

Your account

Where do I log in?

Log in at portal.sharewifiqr.com, or use Log in at the top of any page. That is where you create, edit and download codes and see their scan counts.

What happens if I change my Wi-Fi password?

For a direct code, create a new code and print it again, because the old password is inside it. For a tracked code, update the password in the portal and the printed code keeps working.
