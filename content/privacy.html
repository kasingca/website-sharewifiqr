Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information Share Wifi QR collects when you visit sharewifiqr.com, use the portal at portal.sharewifiqr.com, or scan a Share Wifi QR code, and how we use it.

Information you give us

  • Account details, such as your email address, when you sign up for the portal
  • The network name, password and security type you enter for tracked codes, so we can show them to people who scan the code
  • Billing details for paid plans, which are handled by our payment provider

Information we collect when a tracked code is scanned

When someone scans a tracked code, we record that a scan happened and when. We use this to show scan counts in the code owner's portal. We don't ask the person scanning for their name, email or phone number.

Direct codes

A direct code contains the network details inside the QR image. Scanning it does not contact our servers, so we receive no information when a direct code is scanned.

This website

sharewifiqr.com collects anonymous performance measurements to keep pages fast. It does not use advertising cookies.

How we use information

  • To provide the service: creating codes, showing network details, and counting scans
  • To keep your account secure and to contact you about it
  • To improve the service

We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep account information while your account is open. When you delete a tracked code, its network details are deleted. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Your choices

You can view, change or delete your codes and account details in the portal. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights to access or delete your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
